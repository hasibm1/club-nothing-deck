The Event

Our two collectives host regular dub & reggae sessions through September & October, leading up to the Club Nothing × Keep Hush Live nights on October 9th and 16th.

2 wks

Daytime sound-system sessions · weekend club nights

The programming centres on artists from across South Asia who are independently building bass scenes in their own regions — and who rarely get to share a stage.

The goal is to connect the makers and practitioners of these scenes, to foster knowledge sharing and create pathways to bigger collaborations in the future.

Budget

Total project cost is $11,382. The grant is concentrated on the 2 Keep Hush Live nights — covering international flights, 2 nights of accommodation, and fair artist fees for touring artists. The 2 additional daytime-session and local club nights are run as a give-back to the community, funded separately by Bhai Bhai and Everest. Bar and food are run by the venue and sit outside this budget.
